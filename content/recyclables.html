Recyclables

We accept various materials which may not be included in the list below. Don't hesitate to call and check for any material you may have.

Please ensure that

  • Your material is cleaned and sorted before you arrive
  • You have picture ID in case asked for it
  • You arrive with enough time to unload before we close
